SHARKS

Sharks and rays have been around for more than 400 million years—long before the dinosaurs—and have survived multiple mass extinctions. Today, there are over 1,000 species of sharks and rays, with scientists continuing to discover new ones each year. Although sharks are often portrayed as dangerous apex predators—and some are— the group is incredibly diverse. Species range from tiny to enormous, occupy different levels of the food chain, and inhabit nearly every corner of the ocean.

Facts about Sharks: Sharks are ancient fish with skeletons made of flexible cartilage instead of bone.

  • No true bones: A shark's body framework consists of cartilage,the same flexible material found in human ears and noses.
  • Older than trees: Sharks first appeared in the ocean more than 400 million years ago, predating both land trees and dinosaurs.
  • Sandpaper skin: Their skin is covered in tiny, tooth-like scales called dermal denticles that reduce water friction.
